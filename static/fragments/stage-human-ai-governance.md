# Stage: human-led AI governance

Read `references/human-led-ai-collaboration.md` and `references/ai-and-competition-compliance.md`.

## Procedure

1. Verify the active official participation, paper-format, and AI-tool rules.
2. Label every instruction as an official requirement, quality practice, or team policy.
3. Allocate roles using the responsibility matrix; keep core decisions under team authority.
4. Open the AI-use log at the first material interaction rather than reconstructing it at submission.
5. Record every AI system separately. For an overnight external baseline, preserve the tool/version,
   key interaction log, returned artifacts, actual modeling route, adopted content, and manual
   changes. Do not treat departure from an earlier AI recommendation as noncompliance.
6. Use the decision gates for problem interpretation, model choice, external-baseline release for audit, result acceptance, and manuscript sign-off.
7. Mark artifacts as `AI-proposed`, `AI-recommended`, `team-approved`, or `verified`.
8. Red-team the submission for fabricated facts, unverifiable citations, hidden manual steps, unsupported originality claims, and undisclosed AI content.

## Required internal artifacts

- completed `assets/templates/核心建模决策记录模板.md`;
- current AI-use log, `AI工具使用详情内部清单.json`, and PDF disclosure mapping;
- unresolved decisions and assigned human owner;
- a short oral-defense question set covering every core model.

Do not claim that a team member wrote, checked, understood, or independently derived material unless that action was actually performed.
