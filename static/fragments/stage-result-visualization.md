# Stage: result visualization

Read `references/visualization-standards.md` and `references/writing-template.md`.

## Procedure

1. Map each requested output to one definitive table, figure, formula, or sentence.
2. Report units, precision justified by input accuracy, uncertainty, and applicable scenario.
3. Prefer plots that compare baseline, proposed model, observed values, uncertainty, constraints, or policy tradeoffs.
4. Annotate thresholds, optima, infeasible regions, and changes of optimal regime.
5. Explain the mechanism behind each important pattern; do not merely narrate curves.
6. Separate diagnostic plots from final-answer plots.
7. Ensure the text states the direct answer even when the figure is present.
8. Have the team verify the numerical content and approve the mechanism or decision interpretation; record material AI-generated figures and prose in the AI-use log.

## Minimum final set

- one model/algorithm flow figure;
- one data or mechanism diagnostic;
- one main result figure or table per subproblem;
- one validation figure;
- one robustness or strategy-boundary figure when applicable.

Do not use decorative 3D effects, rainbow palettes, unreadable heatmaps, screenshots of tables, or unexplained precision.
