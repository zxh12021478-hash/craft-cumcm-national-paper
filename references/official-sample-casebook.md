# Official sample-paper casebook

Use this as a routing aid, then open the cited official display page before relying on details. Extract patterns; do not copy content. Treat “officially displayed” and “national first/second prize” as different claims: never assign a prize grade unless the official page or award list explicitly states it.

The machine-readable index covers every official year landing page from 2012 onward and every individual paper link discoverable from those pages. A `landing-only` year means the official year page is still indexed even when its individual items cannot be reliably extracted; open that landing page when the year is relevant.

## 2023

- **A, heliostat field:** focus on decomposed optical efficiency, solar geometry, layout/size coupling, and scalable spatial optimization.
- **B, multibeam survey lines:** focus on seabed geometry, coverage/overlap constraints, track-direction choice, and extending from ideal planes to measured terrain.
- **C, vegetable pricing and replenishment:** focus on temporal demand, price-demand interaction, hierarchical category/item decisions, spoilage, rolling validation, and decision profit rather than prediction alone.
- Official year display: https://dxs.moe.gov.cn/zx/hd/sxjm/sxjmlw/2023qgdxssxjmjslwzs/
- **Award-verified B anchor:** the Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications team won
  national first and North Tai star. Its journal paper proves the regular-slope direction first,
  then partitions irregular terrain by fit quality and recombines local layouts. The official
  display code was not established and remains null. Identity and award chain:
  `E2023-B-JMMIA-01-10`.

## Questions to ask of every sample

1. What was the baseline formulation?
2. What structural insight reduced or reframed the problem?
3. What assumptions were genuinely tested?
4. How were results independently validated?
5. Did uncertainty affect the decision?
6. Which visual carried the main argument?
7. What should not be copied because it is problem-specific?
