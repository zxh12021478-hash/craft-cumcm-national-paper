# AI and competition compliance

Rules can change. Before a current competition, verify the official CUMCM website and cite the active documents. Do not rely solely on this file.

## Verified status on 2026-08-19

The official 2026 participation rules permit AI tools as assistance and make each team responsible
for the originality, authenticity, and accuracy of its submission. They incorporate the official
AI-tool rules.

The 2026 AI-tool rule published on 2026-08-03 takes effect on 2026-09-01. It requires the team to
lead the core modeling and analysis and to review and verify AI-assisted content item by item. The
paper must place an `AI工具使用声明` before the references, using the official used-AI or no-AI
wording. When AI was used, the supporting materials must contain `AI工具使用详情.pdf` with the
tool/model, purpose and stage, prompting approach and process, and the main adoption, human
modification, and verification details. Unlike the superseded 2025 rule, the 2026 rule does not
require corresponding-position AI markings or an AI-tool reference entry.

Recheck immediately before and during the competition for a superseding notice.

## Baseline obligations to verify

- independent team work and no prohibited communication;
- anonymity and group eligibility;
- page, file-size, format, and submission limits;
- complete runnable code and supporting-material consistency;
- citation of all external facts, methods, data, and code;
- originality and similarity thresholds;
- current AI-use declaration and supporting PDF requirements.

Under the active 2026 AI-tool rule, using AI is permitted with transparency while the team leads
core modeling and analysis and reviews and verifies AI-assisted content item by item. Put the
official `AI工具使用声明` before the references. If AI was used, include a PDF named
`AI工具使用详情.pdf` in the supporting materials and record the tool/model, purpose and stage,
prompting approach and process, and the main adoption, human modification, and verification
details. Preserve timestamps, providers, affected locations, and fuller interaction records as
team traceability policy, not as invented official fields. Recheck for a superseding notice before
submission.

## Claims that are not official rules unless a new notice says otherwise

- AI assistance is “fully unrestricted.”
- Every AI-written paragraph or every AI-suggested citation is categorically banned.
- A particular writing style or failed oral explanation automatically proves AI misuse.
- Runtime screenshots are mandatory proof of code authorship.
- A fixed similarity or AI-detection score determines disqualification.

Treat these as possible coaching heuristics or risk signals, not official requirements. Whole-paper
outsourcing is incompatible with the team-led core-modeling requirement and the item-by-item human
review duty even though the official notice does not enumerate every prohibited drafting pattern.

## AI-use log

Record each material interaction:

- timestamp and team stage;
- tool, model, version, and provider;
- purpose;
- prompt or faithful key excerpt;
- material response excerpt or artifact reference;
- adopted/rejected status;
- human checks performed;
- modifications made;
- affected paper/code locations.

## Integrity boundary

AI may assist where rules allow, but the team must inspect, understand, independently verify, and accept core decisions. Never hide AI use, invent verification, or describe generated content as independently human-derived.

Never help a team disguise AI provenance, manipulate phrasing to evade detection, or backfill fictional human work. Improve compliance by recording actual interactions, checks, decisions, and modifications.

## Sample-paper boundary

Use official displayed papers to learn structure, reasoning patterns, validation, and presentation. Do not copy language, equations, figures, code, or problem-specific formulations. Cite any borrowed method from its original scholarly source, not merely from a competition paper.
