## Summary

On twelve held-out synthetic evaluation days, we compare the reference dispatch policy P with a refinement R that changes the timing of a reserve release; configurations were frozen before the evaluation days were generated, and each policy used identical tuning budgets, feature histories, stopping rules and cost definitions. R lowers total complete operating cost by 5% (CNY 11,400 versus 12,000) and raises aggregate service from 94% to 96% (2 percentage points), with all twelve paired days favoring R on both measures. Both policies complete every day, satisfy all recorded hard constraints, and remain above the 90% daily service floor on every day. R is therefore recommended over P for this tested set of synthetic days. This is a paired, day-level descriptive comparison: it does not establish statistical significance, a probability of future feasibility, generalization beyond the synthetic generator, or real implementation performance, and R's reserve-release equation remains to be reviewed from the actual implementation.
