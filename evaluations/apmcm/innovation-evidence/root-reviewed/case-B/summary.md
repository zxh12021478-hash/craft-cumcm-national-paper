## Summary

A dispatch refinement R changes the timing of reserve release and is compared with reference policy P on twelve paired synthetic evaluation days. R reduces complete operating cost by 5%, from CNY 12,000 to 11,400, and raises service from 94% to 96%, a gain of 2 percentage points. Both policies satisfy all recorded hard constraints and exceed the 90% service floor on every day. We recommend R over P for this tested set. The supplied records support this descriptive comparison; implementation fidelity and performance beyond the synthetic evaluation remain unverified.
