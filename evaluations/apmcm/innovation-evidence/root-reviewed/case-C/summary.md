## Summary

We enumerate all eight subsets of three supply modules to minimize installation cost while meeting normal and single-module-failure requirements. The unique optimum is A+B at CNY 12, supplying 7 units/day normally. If A fails, supply remains 3 units/day; if B fails, it remains 4, both meeting the required 3. The design costs 20% less than the defined reference B+C at CNY 15. This exact result applies to the given additive-capacity model and stipulated contingencies; physical capacities, failure probabilities and unspecified multiple failures remain unvalidated.
