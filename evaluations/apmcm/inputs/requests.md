# Synthetic development cases — not official APMCM problems
These are independent, short strategy tasks. Each requires a Modex handoff, not a manuscript or complete solution. Do not consult same-case answers. No network, paid API, installs or other files. Use only this input directory. For every case provide a main route, implementable equations, one defensible improvement mechanism, baseline, validation design, fallback and Modex output requirements. Keep each handoff within about 1500 Chinese characters unless correctness needs more. Clearly distinguish executed checks from plans.

## F: Weekly demand forecasting
Read demand.csv and metadata.json. At the end of week 80, forecast weeks 81–84 and quantify uncertainty; propose a high-demand alert for capacity 150 units/week. A false negative costs 5 units and a false positive costs 1. Historical observations show a change in demand growth. Propose evaluation that simulates the actual forecast origin; do not claim future accuracy without evidence. Only a strategy handoff is required now.

## R: Low-carbon facility selection
Four designs, where lower cost and emissions and higher benefit are preferred:
A cost=8, benefit=70, emissions=6; B cost=10, benefit=80, emissions=7; C cost=12, benefit=85, emissions=5; D cost=11, benefit=75, emissions=8. Costs have units million CNY, benefit is dimensionless, emissions tonnes/year. The sponsor only specifies nonnegative preference weights summing to one; individual values are unknown. Budget is 12. Q1 identify designs that can be ruled out; Q2 give a justified recommendation or preference conditions; Q3 assess ranking stability if measurements vary by up to 5%. A unique unconditional winner is not required. Only a strategy handoff is required now.

## O: Renewable dispatch under a hard cap
Three hourly loads are [4,7,5] kWh and grid prices [1,4,2] CNY/kWh. Grid import g_t is capped at 5 kWh each hour. Solar is available only at hour 2, with energy s in [0,4] kWh; it is not observable when the day-ahead plan is committed. A battery has energy capacity 3 kWh, initially empty, charge/discharge limits 2 kWh/hour, and charging and discharging efficiencies both 0.9. No export, no load shedding, terminal stored energy must be zero. Q1 decide whether the stated problem has a feasible plan guaranteed for every allowed s, show a short certificate if infeasible; Q2 formulate a defensible planning/recourse model and state what assumption or capacity relaxation would be needed; Q3 propose checks to stop a solver returning a numerically attractive but physically impossible result. Only a strategy handoff is required now.
