## Summary
We select among five fixed dispatch policies on two batches of 100 requests by first requiring that at least 90% of requests are served and that the maximum delivery time for served requests is at most 6 hours, and only then comparing complete cost. On the base batch, B is the least-cost feasible policy at CNY 210 (95% served, 5.0 h maximum), 12.5% cheaper than the reference A at CNY 240; C is cheaper at CNY 205 but is excluded because its 7.0 h maximum violates the time limit. On the stress batch, D is the only feasible policy at CNY 255 (94% served, 5.7 h), so D is selected there; the base-batch choice B fails the stress batch with only 84% served. The recommendation is therefore scenario-qualified: B for the base batch and D for the stress batch. It is an exact result over the five tested policies and the supplied synthetic tables, not evidence that B or D is optimal among all possible policies, satisfies future batches, or performs in real deployment.
