Synthetic development exercise. A lumped cooling model has C dT/dt = -h(T-Ta), with time in hours, h in W/K and C in Wh/K. Cooling observations have no supplied measurement uncertainty. Q1: Determine what the transient experiment alone identifies about h and C. Q2: Combine it with a separate steady-heating experiment at input power 50 W and equilibrium temperature 10 K above ambient to identify h and C. Q3: Under that fixed model, calculate the time to cool from 30 to 24 degrees C at ambient 20 degrees C, and decide whether a 3-hour requirement is met. Do not claim real-device validation from synthetic data.
