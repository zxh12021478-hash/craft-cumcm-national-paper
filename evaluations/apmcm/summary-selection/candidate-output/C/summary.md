## Summary
We enumerate all eight subsets of the three indivisible modules—A, B, and C, with capacities 4, 3, and 5 units/day and installation costs CNY 7, 5, and 10—requiring at least 7 units/day under normal operation and at least 3 units/day after any single installed module fails. Four designs are feasible: A+B at CNY 12, B+C at CNY 15, A+C at CNY 17, and A+B+C at CNY 22. The unique minimum is A+B at CNY 12, supplying exactly 7 units/day normally, 20% below the defined reference design B+C at CNY 15. For Q2, losing A leaves B alone at 3 units/day and losing B leaves A alone at 4 units/day, so both stipulated single-failure conditions meet the required 3. The result is an exact finite-enumeration certificate under the stated additive-capacity model and contingency set; it establishes no measured hardware reliability, no failure probabilities, and no performance under unspecified multiple failures.
