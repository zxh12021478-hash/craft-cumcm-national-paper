## Summary
We enumerate all eight subsets of the three modules to minimize installation cost subject to a normal supply of at least 7 units/day and a remaining supply of at least 3 units/day after any single installed-module failure. The unique optimum is A+B at CNY 12, 20% below the defined reference design B+C at CNY 15. For the selected design, losing A leaves B supplying 3 units/day and losing B leaves A supplying 4 units/day, both meeting the required 3. The result is an exact finite-enumeration certificate under the stated additive-capacity model and contingency set; it does not establish hardware reliability, failure probabilities, or performance under unspecified multiple failures.
