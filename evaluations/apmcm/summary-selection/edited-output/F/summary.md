## Summary
We select among five fixed dispatch policies on two batches of 100 requests, applying feasibility first—at least 90% of requests served and a maximum delivery time of at most 6 hours for served requests—and comparing complete cost only afterward. On the base batch, B is the least-cost feasible policy at CNY 210, 12.5% cheaper than the reference A at CNY 240; C is cheaper at CNY 205 but is excluded because its 7.0 h maximum violates the time limit. On the stress batch, B fails with only 84% served and D is the only feasible policy at CNY 255, giving a scenario-qualified recommendation: B for the base batch and D for the stress batch. These are exact results over the five tested policies and the supplied synthetic tables, not evidence that B or D is optimal among all possible policies, satisfies future batches, or performs in real deployment.
