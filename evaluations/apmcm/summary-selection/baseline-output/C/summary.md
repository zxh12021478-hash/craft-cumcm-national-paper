## Summary

We choose a minimum-cost subset of three indivisible supply modules — A (capacity 4 units/day, cost CNY 7), B (3 units/day, CNY 5) and C (5 units/day, CNY 10) — requiring at least 7 units/day under normal operation and at least 3 units/day after the loss of any one installed module; capacities add without losses. Enumerating all 2³ = 8 subsets with integer arithmetic shows exactly four feasible designs: A+B at CNY 12, B+C at CNY 15, A+C at CNY 17 and A+B+C at CNY 22. The unique minimum-cost design is A+B (Q1), supplying 7 units/day normally. Under its two single-module failures (Q2), remaining supply is 3 units/day if A fails (B alone) and 4 units/day if B fails (A alone), so the worst stipulated post-failure supply is exactly 3. Relative to the defined reference design B+C at CNY 15, A+B costs 20% less. This is an exact finite-enumeration certificate for the stated additive-capacity model and contingency set; it does not establish measured hardware reliability, failure probabilities, capacity under unstated multiple failures, or performance beyond the stipulated conditions.
