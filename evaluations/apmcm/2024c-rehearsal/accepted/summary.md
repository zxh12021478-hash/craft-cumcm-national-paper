# Summary

We separate pet-population forecasts, food-demand accounting and policy scenarios to analyze China's pet industry without treating monetary values as physical quantities. For 2024–2026, the selected simple extrapolators forecast China's cats at 77.622, 84.432 and 91.242 million, and dogs at 50.657, 49.939 and 49.231 million. Model selection uses only two chronological one-step holdouts; this does not establish three-year forecast accuracy, causal drivers or real-time data availability.

For the United States, France and Germany, fixed but unknown nonnegative per-animal food coefficients give a 2026/2023 demand ratio between 0.8973 and 1.0679, conditional on the pet-count point forecasts. Thus these data do not determine even the direction of demand growth across the three markets. This bound is neither global nor a confidence interval. Separately, a preliminary 2023 global production anchor of 34.96 million tonnes yields consumption scenarios of 35.974, 37.773 and 39.661 million tonnes if production grows 5% annually, losses are 2% and inventories are unchanged; a zero-growth baseline remains 34.261 million tonnes annually. These assumptions are unestimated.

China's pet-food production-value forecasts are CNY 305.019, 359.872 and 414.725 billion; exports remain USD 3.96 billion annually under the selected last-value baseline. Converting the 2019 export entry from CNY using its verified annual exchange rate avoids exaggerating the observed 2019–2020 export decline. For an illustrative tariff rise from 10% to 25%, unit-elastic demand reduces affected-market export revenue by 12% regardless of producer absorption, while profit still depends on absorption and unit cost. Mitigation is worthwhile only below the attainable restored profit.

The reproducible outputs provide conditional decisions and explicit data gaps. Limited historical observations, unverified release dates, uncertain global demand and uncalibrated policy parameters prevent a claim of validated predictive performance or a completed competition-ready solution.
