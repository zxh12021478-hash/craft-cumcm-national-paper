# Historical rehearsal protocol

Use official problem files and their original attachments. Record URLs, SHA-256, start time, allowed resources, and pre-exposure ledger before reading same-case material. General documentation and product specifications are allowed; same-case code, winning papers and solution posts are not allowed until strategy freeze. Historical problems may be familiar to a model: call this solution-isolated historical rehearsal, never zero-knowledge or prospective testing.

1. Inventory every official file. Freeze a requirement matrix, including numeric constraints, all subquestions, external-data needs and mandatory output files. Choose and record the primary task family after reading the question.
2. Compare a simple baseline and genuinely different routes. Write and hash a strategy with assumptions and falsification checks before coding. Do not force a model count or claim innovation from combining familiar algorithms.
3. Run deterministic experiments. State units, parameter provenance, initial and boundary conditions. Save the tested candidate set or search space and optimization diagnostics. Compare fairly against the same resources and constraints. Keep estimated versus measured quantities explicit.
4. Independently check high-consequence outputs: analytic limit, separate implementation, conservation, brute force on a small instance, or dimensional/constraint identity. Stability/convergence studies and sensitivity must reach the recommendation, not sit in an unused appendix.
5. Generate an English paper, figures and result ledger from the executed outputs. Cover every required scenario and output; label model/data limitations explicitly. Render and inspect the final PDF. Use historical official formatting only for this historical rehearsal.
6. Re-run in a clean output directory. Compare headline values and artifact hashes where deterministic. Produce a local evidence ZIP containing the paper, runnable code, derived results and verification records. Do not upload to a live contest or manufacture a signed control sheet.
7. Give frozen artifacts and the problem to an independent AI assessor without expected findings. Separate self-assessment, independent AI findings and real human feedback. Repair confirmed defects and rerun affected checks. Status stays incomplete if required evidence remains missing.

Assessment dimensions (0–4, with evidence): requirement coverage, model validity, reproducibility, independent verification, numerical/English/figure consistency, useful structural insight, and decision value. A missing required answer, unreproduced headline, material constraint error, or mislabeled contamination is a failed case regardless of average. No score translates to a prize probability. Record token use only if available; record elapsed time honestly.
